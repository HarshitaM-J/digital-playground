Your level briefing

Your first website starts here.

Turn an idea into a website a classmate can open and use.

01

Give the AI a brief, not a mystery.

Tell it who the site is for, what it should do and how it should look. “A course journal for classmates, with session tabs and quizzes” is a stronger starting point than “make it cool.”

02

Give each file a job.

Picture a quiz button. HTML puts it on the page. CSS gives it colour and a pixel shadow. JavaScript decides what happens when you press it.

03

Take your build live.

Build and test your files. Save them to GitHub for version history. Connect that repository to Vercel, deploy, then check the public link. That’s your launch sequence.

    ✦Building an AI Marketing Team

    30 October 2026 · A lightweight agentic workflow with Claude

    ✦Nick GohYellow Pages Singapore↗
    Level briefing

    Can your campaign make it to launch?

    Nick Goh used a hypothetical Louis Vuitton campaign to show how specialised AI roles can work together in Claude. You’ll play the human lead who reviews their work and decides when it’s ready.

    Set up your campaign HQ

    A Claude Project holds the team’s instructions and references. A Marketing Brief Builder skill makes the task repeatable. An email or scheduled trigger starts the draft.

    Lecture slides 7–15

    Put the planning squad to work

    Brief Writer
    Drafts from the project context. The goal, audience, budget and timing give the team something concrete to work with.
    Brief Validator
    Opens a fresh chat to check the objective, audience, message and metric. Hands back a verdict and the most important fixes.
    Strategist
    Uses the validated brief, audience and category research to shape the campaign’s central idea. Hands over a Big Idea one-pager.
    Human lead
    Approves the direction and saves it in the project knowledge base before the creative team starts.

    Lecture slides 4, 6 and 14–20

    Send the idea into production

    Designer / Art Director
    Turns the approved idea into key visual concepts and assets. Nick demonstrated a connection to Higgsfield for visual production.
    Copywriter
    Builds a messaging hierarchy, then adapts the copy to each channel. In the Terra case, the Story, feed caption and other channel treatments had different jobs.
    Reviewer
    Checks the work against the brief, Big Idea and brand voice. The output is an edit list or sign-off.

    Lecture slides 5–6 and 21–45

    Lecture case study

    Terra, a hypothetical Louis Vuitton campaign

    The brief asked the team to turn curiosity about Terra into interest in visiting a Singapore boutique. Boutique appointment enquiries were one way to tell if it worked. Anything the draft said about environmental benefits needed evidence and approval before it could go live.

    In one image, a hand about to open the clasp created anticipation. The reviewer suggested widening the crop to show the bag’s shape and leave room for the call to action.

    Level 03 / Digital Platforms

    inLinkedIn: SEO to GEO

    13 November 2026 · Connect & Influence Through LinkedIn

    inCindy LeongLinkedIn Marketing Solutions↗
    Level briefing

    Would you trust this enough to cite it?

    Your buyers may be months away from a decision. In Cindy Leong’s session, the goal is to earn their trust early and make your expertise easy to find when they start asking questions.

    Be useful before the buyer is ready

    LinkedIn grew from a jobs site into a professional feed where people hire, market, learn and sell. Build your audience strategy around people whose work makes the topic relevant. The 95:5 rule is a B2B planning principle: most potential buyers aren’t in the market right now. Be useful early so they remember you when the need arrives.

    A company page can publish official information. Founders, employees and specialists can show how they solved real problems, add context in comments, and build a reputation readers can recognise over time.

    FOMU vs FOMO. In a buying group, fear of making the wrong decision can outweigh fear of missing out. The slide cites Forrester for the figure that 40% of deals stall when the group can’t agree. Give people a pilot, a relevant case study or a clear comparison they can bring to their colleagues.

    Give good answers a better chance of being found

    SEO (search engine optimisation) helps people find pages in search results. GEO (generative engine optimisation) aims to make content easier for AI systems to understand and potentially cite in a generated answer. Someone may learn about your brand from the answer before they ever visit your site.

    Start with a real audience question. Answer it early, use descriptive headings and name the specific product or topic. Add evidence and first-hand examples, keep facts current, and include a transcript when a video carries the main explanation.

    AI citations in the classroom analysis. The slide attributed 40% to members with 10,000 or more followers, 35% to members with fewer than 10,000, and 25% to company pages. The Meltwater figures are directional and vary by prompt and AI system. Smaller accounts appeared in the results too.

    Show your workings

    Experience
    Show what happened when you did the work yourself.
    Expertise
    Make the author’s relevant knowledge and credentials visible.
    Authoritativeness
    Give readers a way to find respected independent sources that recognise the work.
    Trustworthiness
    Link to evidence, disclose limitations, and correct claims that change.

    These E-E-A-T ideas help you assess credibility. Clear, credible writing can support discovery, and citation depends on the system and the query.

    Give the right post a push, then measure what matters

    A short post can start a conversation. An article can unpack a question in more depth. Thought Leader Ads can amplify eligible posts from individuals, so use them to extend the reach of work that already has something worth saying.

    Measure against your goal. Impressions show exposure, relevant comments signal engagement, and qualified enquiries measure a different outcome. An AI answer citing your article is a discovery signal. Track it separately from traffic and leads.

    LinkedIn by the numbers · November 2026

    Platform figures shown in class.

    • 1.3B+ members worldwide, 427M+ in APAC and 5.5M in Singapore. The slide showed more than three members joining per second.
    • APAC examples: India 210M, China 59M, Indonesia 45M, Australia 19M and Japan 6.1M.
    • Professional reach: 130M+ decision-makers, 14M+ C-suite executives and 71M companies.
    • Trust figures: 100M verified member profiles and a reported brand safety score above 99%.

    Classroom slides photographed by Sasha Leonardi

    Try it on a real post

    Take one question your audience actually asks. Write a direct answer, add one example from your own work and link to one source. If you’re writing for a buying group, give your reader a fact or comparison they can pass on to a colleague.
